CASE 01 / DESIGN REASONING

LINA — from literal UI to cinematic storytelling.

INITIAL DIRECTION

Clear information, limited emotional depth.

Lina exits the corporate tower.

Her phone vibrates.

214 MATCHES → 31 MATCHES → 0 MATCHES

Lina stops.

Diagnosis: the audience can read the rejection, but the scene asks UI text to carry the emotional weight. The social isolation is stated rather than visually experienced.

→

REFINED DIRECTION

Use space, movement, contrast, and reaction.

  1. 01Front medium close-up — hold Lina’s disappointment against the corporate tower.
  2. 02Move from frontal to profile as she walks; crane upward until her figure dissolves into the city crowd.
  3. 03Cut to Lina seated outside while confident professionals pass with coffee, actively discussing work.
  4. 04Low 45° angle — Lina checks her phone; let sadness shift into surprise through performance, not explanatory text.

Design decision: convert a status update into environmental storytelling. The frame now communicates hierarchy, exclusion, pace, and emotion before the phone screen is understood.

EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

How I review visual output.

01

Composition

Does the frame establish a clear subject, spatial relationship, and intentional balance?

02

Hierarchy

Does the viewer know where to look first, second, and why?

03

Continuity

Do character, environment, props, direction, and screen geography remain coherent across shots?

04

Emotional Clarity

Is the intended feeling carried by performance and visual decisions rather than exposition alone?

05

Consistency

Do styling, materials, lighting, typography, and generated details belong to the same visual system?

06

Production Fitness

Is the output only visually impressive, or is it usable, repeatable, editable, and appropriate for the final format?
